# Passive Income Ideas

A collection of ideas for generating passive income.

---

## Ideas

1. **Sell coloring books on Amazon** — Create and sell coloring books for kids and adults on Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing).

2. **YouTube channel with long-form educational/entertaining videos** — Topics: interesting science facts, animal world, classical music, funny videos.

3. **AI model / social media influencer** — Create an AI-generated persona or virtual influencer on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.

4. **AI-powered product websites** — Websites that sell easy-to-generate AI products, such as:
   - Wedding invitations generated by AI
   - Custom pet photos to print
   - Upload a photo and get it shipped on canvas
   - Personalized story books created from prompts
   - Animated videos for job interview prep (riddles, common questions, etc.)

5. **Wedding planning app** — An app to help couples plan their wedding (budget tracking, vendor management, guest lists, etc.).

6. **3D printer connector app** — A platform that connects people who own 3D printers with people who need something printed.

7. **Pregnancy assistance app** — An app for expectant mothers with: week-by-week tracking, test reminders and explanations, baby size updates, coupons, stroller suggestions, and more.

8. **RPG game combining popular game universes** — A game that blends elements from World of Warcraft, Pokemon, Elder Scrolls, and similar games. Could start simpler — a 2D game where you move around, kill monsters, level up fast, and gain perks (inspired by games like Azeroth Survivor).

9. **Smart calendar alarm assistant app** — An app that monitors your calendar and:
   - Alerts you if your alarm is set less than X hour(s) before your first scheduled event
   - Notifies you if you forgot to set an alarm at all
   - Automatically sets an alarm X hour(s) before your first calendar item if you still haven't set one

10. **Outdoor escape room app** — An app that turns a real-world location (like a park) into an escape room experience. Supports personalization using info from family/friends for custom riddles and questions — great for birthdays and events.

11. **Habit building app** — An app that helps users build and maintain good habits over time.
